### Description (4000 chars max)

```
Localese teaches you the Indian language your city speaks — the one that gets you better service, warmer conversations, and a real sense of belonging.

Built for real life, not textbooks.

Whether you've just moved to Hyderabad, landed a job in Bengaluru, or are finally visiting family in Kolkata — Localese gives you the phrases that actually matter. Ordering at an Irani chai hotel. Catching an auto. Thanking a colleague. Making small talk at a wedding. Understanding what your landlord is saying.

No grammar drills. No verb conjugation tables. Just the language of daily life, taught fast.

──────────────────────────
WHAT MAKES LOCALESE DIFFERENT
──────────────────────────

🎧 Native audio on everything
Every word, every phrase, every example sentence is spoken by a real voice. Tap 🔊 on any flashcard or phrase in the Reference guide to hear it instantly. No phonetic guesswork, no silent app.

📚 Teach → Practice → Quiz
Each lesson has three phases. First you study new words with audio flashcards. Then you practice them with guided exercises — no penalty for mistakes. Then you're tested. You build real memory, not just passive recognition.

🗺️ City-ready from day one
Every unit is rooted in a real Indian city. Hindi for Delhi's metro crowd and Mumbai's offices. Telugu for Hyderabad's tech corridors. Kannada for Bengaluru's startup scene. Marathi for Mumbai's local trains and Pune's campuses. Bengali for Kolkata's culture and commerce.

💬 A full reference guide — always free
600+ phrases across 13 categories: greetings, numbers, food, shopping, transport, directions, workplace, emergencies and more. Browse any time, even mid-conversation. Tap any phrase to hear it spoken.

  Spoken by 600M+ across North India

  One of the world's most literary languages

• Marathi — Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Nagpur
  The proud language of India's financial capital

──────────────────────────
COMPLETELY FREE
──────────────────────────

All 4 stages, all 32 lessons per language, and the full Reference guide — completely free. No account needed. No subscription. No ads. Just open the app and start learning.

Privacy policy: https://localese.app/privacy
```

## App Review Notes (paste into Notes field in App Store Connect)

```
Localese is a free Indian language-learning app for professionals who've moved cities in India.

REVIEWER NOTES:
- No login required. Tap "Get started" on the welcome screen → pick a language → learning begins immediately.
- All 4 stages and all lessons are fully unlocked — no paywall, no account, no purchase required.
- Audio works via pre-generated MP3 files served from CDN. Tap the 🔊 button on any flashcard or phrase in the Reference tab to hear it.
- The app requires no special permissions — no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts.
- User progress is stored locally on-device only (no account, no server sync).
- The app has been tested on iPhone 15 Pro running iOS 26.5.

TEST FLOW:
1. Launch app → tap "Get started" → pick "Hindi"
2. Tap Stage 1 → Unit 1 → "Arrival Essentials" → Start
3. Complete the flashcard deck → tap through guided practice → complete quiz
4. Return to Learn page → tap Reference tab → tap any category → tap 🔊 on any phrase
```
